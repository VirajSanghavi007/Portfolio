Hi, I'm Viraj.

I'm a third-year Computer Engineering student at DJ Sanghavi College of Engineering (graduating 2028), building systems across graph ML, edge ML, Federated Learning research and business automation. I didn't wait for a degree to do real work: two internships of production AI and automation, a graph AML detector, a CIFAR-10 model squeezed into 70 KB, and research at DJS Anveshan on why Federated Learning breaks under attack.

Most of what I build gets re-evaluated harder than a demo needs: walk-forward backtests instead of a single split, error diagnosis instead of trusting one number, drift monitoring instead of assuming a model stays good. I'd rather ship something slower that holds up than something fast that only looks finished.
